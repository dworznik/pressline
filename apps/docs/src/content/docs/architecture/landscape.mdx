Who and what stands around Pressline. Three people: the Customer, who makes a Design in an Engine and buys it printed; the Operator, who runs the one instance and owns the Printful and Stripe accounts; the Engine developer, who builds the Engine, often the same person as the Operator. Pressline talks to roles, not vendors: a Fulfillment Provider, a PSP and a Mailer, each with one shipped implementation. The Engine renders and hosts every Printfile and Preview; Pressline never touches pixels.
